### Lodge 10.25" Cast Iron Skillet — Research Complete
- Report completed: 2026-05-30
- Status: Staple post candidate — keyword approval pending
- Article planned: Lodge 10.25" Cast Iron Skillet Review (2026)

**1. Product Name**
Lodge 10.25-Inch Cast Iron Skillet (L8SK3)

**2. Price + Date**
$24.90 — verified May 30, 2026

**3. Stars + Review Count**
4.7★ — 164,000+ verified Amazon reviews (May 2026)

**4. Key Specs**
- Material: cast iron (PFAS-free, PFOA-free, PTFE-free — no synthetic coating)
- Size: 10.25 inches
- Weight: 5.35 lbs
- Pre-seasoned with 100% natural vegetable oil
- Oven safe to 500°F
- Compatible: gas, electric, induction, oven, campfire
- Made in USA (South Pittsburg, Tennessee)
- Handle: one long + one helper handle

**5. Top 3 Competitors**
- Lodge 12" Cast Iron Skillet: 4.7★ / 80,000+ reviews / ~$35 — larger size, same brand trust
- Victoria 10" Cast Iron Skillet: 4.6★ / 15,000+ reviews / ~$30 — smoother surface, slightly pricier
- AmazonBasics Pre-Seasoned Cast Iron: 4.5★ / 8,000+ reviews / ~$22 — budget option, less seasoning history

**6. Buyer Loves**
- Virtually indestructible — buyers report 10, 20, 30+ year ownership with the same pan
- Gets better with use — seasoning improves over time, unlike nonstick which degrades
- Extreme versatility — stovetop, oven, grill, campfire, all in one pan
- Unbeatable value — buyers consistently note no comparable pan exists at this price point
- Health-conscious appeal — zero synthetic coating, no PFAS/PFOA/PTFE concerns
- Sear quality — buyers report steakhouse-level crust impossible to achieve with nonstick

**7. Buyer Complaints**
- Heavy at 5.35 lbs — wrist strain for some buyers, especially when full of food
- Handle gets hot — no insulation, requires oven mitt even on stovetop
- Rough surface out of box — some buyers find the factory texture less smooth than vintage cast iron or Victoria pans
- Maintenance learning curve — new buyers sometimes confused by seasoning and cleaning rules
- Reactive with acidic foods — tomato-based dishes can strip seasoning and add metallic taste if cooked too long

**8. Gut Reaction**
Strongest possible Staple post candidate. 164K reviews at $24.90 is an extraordinary signal — this is one of the most-validated products on all of Amazon. The health angle (zero synthetic coating) maps perfectly to KVP's non-toxic micro-niche. Buyer sentiment is overwhelmingly positive with very predictable, addressable complaints. Competition assessment shows no page-1 result leading with the buyer-data angle at this price point — genuine gap for a long-tail keyword approach.

**9. Amazon Customers Say**
Buyers consistently describe the Lodge cast iron skillet as a lifetime purchase that improves with age. The most repeated theme is that it outperforms expensive nonstick pans for searing and browning while eliminating coating-degradation anxiety. Health-conscious buyers specifically cite the absence of synthetic coatings as the primary purchase driver. Common friction points are weight and the learning curve for new cast iron users, but buyers who push through the learning curve report high long-term satisfaction.

**CamelCamelCamel Price Check**
Price stable at $24.90–$26.99 range over 12 months. No significant spikes or drops. Safe to cite ~$24.90 with standard price disclaimer.

**Google Top 5 Competition Assessment**
Head term "best cast iron skillet" dominated by Wirecutter, Serious Eats, Good Housekeeping (DR 79–93). Not targetable for a new site. Long-tail angle "lodge cast iron skillet review" or "lodge 10.25 cast iron review" shows weaker competition — forums, older blog posts, no dominant authority page leading with buyer-data analysis. Gap confirmed.
