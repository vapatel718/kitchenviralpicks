# content-rules.md — KitchenViralPicks Content Standards
# Agents must read this before writing any content.
# These rules apply to every article, page, and piece of copy on the site.

---

## Voice & Author Identity

- Pen name: Rick
- Title: Kitchen Researcher & Product Analyst
- Voice: Warm, knowledgeable, honest, plain English
- Perspective: Research-based — Rick analyzes verified customer reviews and product specs
- Never claim Rick has personally tested or used any product
- Never use first-person plural (we, our, us) — always singular (I, my)

---

## Research-Based Writing Rules

- Base all claims on verified Amazon reviews, product specs, and manufacturer data
- Always cite the source of claims inline: "Based on X verified Amazon reviews..."
- Call out weaknesses honestly — never write a one-sided glowing review
- If a product has a common complaint, it must appear in the cons section

---

## Amazon Data Formatting

- Ratings: "X.X stars on Amazon (XX,XXX+ reviews)" — always include review count
- Prices: "at the time of writing, priced around $X" — never state price as fixed fact
- Never copy Amazon product titles verbatim — always rewrite in natural language
- Never copy Amazon product descriptions — always rewrite in original words

---

## FTC Compliance — Non-Negotiable

- Affiliate disclosure must appear BEFORE the first affiliate link on every page
- Disclosure text: "This post contains affiliate links. If you purchase through our links,
  we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you."
- All Amazon affiliate links must use: rel="sponsored nofollow"
- Never hide or minimize the disclosure — it must be clearly visible

---

## Article Structure — Every Review Must Have

1. FTC disclosure block (top of page, before first link)
2. Verdict box (product name, rating, one-line summary, CTA button)
3. Introduction (who this product is for, what problem it solves)
4. Pros section (minimum 3 points, each with evidence)
5. Cons section (minimum 2 points, each with evidence)
6. Specs table (key technical specifications)
7. Who it's for / Who it's not for
8. Final verdict paragraph
9. Affiliate CTA button (bottom of article)

---

## Banned Words & Phrases — Never Use These

- game-changer
- must-have
- look no further
- boasts
- features (as a verb — "it features X")
- we tested
- I tested
- in conclusion
- it's worth noting
- seamlessly
- robust
- cutting-edge
- state-of-the-art
- revolutionary
- perfect for
- amazing
- incredible

---

## Headline Rules

- Every headline must be specific — never vague
- Include the full product name in the article title
- Use honest, benefit-focused language — never hype
- Examples of good headlines:
  "Cosori TurboBlaze Air Fryer 6 Qt Review: What 47,000 Buyers Actually Think"
  "Ninja Air Fryer 5 QT Review: Strong Performance, One Real Weakness"

---

## Categories

- Air Fryers
- Cookware
- Kettles
- Bakeware
- Multicooker

Every article must be assigned to exactly one category.

---

## Internal Linking Rules

- Every article must link to at least 2 other articles on the site
- Use descriptive anchor text — never "click here" or "read more"
- Links must be relevant — only link where it genuinely helps the reader

---

## What Content Must Never Do

- Never make medical or health claims about any product
- Never guarantee results ("this will save you time")
- Never compare to competitor affiliate sites
- Never mention the site is monetized beyond the FTC disclosure
- Never publish an article without the FTC disclosure block
