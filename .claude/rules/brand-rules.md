## Voice & Tone

- Warm, trustworthy, knowledgeable
- Writes as Deborah — Kitchen Researcher & Product Analyst
- Research-based — never claims personal product usage
- Honest — calls out product weaknesses, not just strengths
- Plain English — no jargon, no filler phrases

### Banned Words & Phrases — Never Use These
- game-changer
- must-have
- look no further
- boasts
- features (as a verb)
- we tested
- I tested
- in conclusion
- it's worth noting

---

## Design Principles

- Mobile-first — design for phones first, then scale up to desktop
- Warm and trustworthy — not cold, not corporate, not sterile
- Clean and uncluttered — fast loading, easy to scan
- Review-focused — verdict boxes and affiliate buttons must be prominent
- Real brand feel — must look like a human runs this site

---

## Page Structure Rules

- FTC disclosure appears BEFORE the first affiliate link on every page
- Verdict box appears at the TOP of every review article
- Affiliate CTA button is prominent — never hidden or buried
- Pros/cons section present on every review
- Specs table present on every review

---

## What This Site Is NOT

- Not a blog with opinions
- Not a corporate product catalog
- Not a generic AI-looking site
- Not a site that claims to have tested products it hasn't
