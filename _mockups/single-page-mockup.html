The problem this solves

If you have ever stood in front of a hot oven in the middle of summer waiting 40 minutes for frozen fries to crisp up, you already understand the appeal of a good air fryer. The challenge is finding one that actually delivers on speed and crispiness without being deafeningly loud, impossible to clean, or so small it only cooks for one person.

The COSORI TurboBlaze 6.0-Quart has quietly become one of the highest-rated air fryers on Amazon — 4.8 stars across more than 19,000 verified reviews at the time of writing. After researching hundreds of those buyer reviews and independent expert tests from outlets like Tom's Guide, TechGearLab, and The Gadgeteer, this guide breaks down exactly what real owners love, what they complain about, and who should and should not buy it.

What this product is

The COSORI TurboBlaze is a 6-quart, single-basket countertop air fryer powered by a DC motor — a newer motor technology that spins a fan at up to 3,600 RPM across five adjustable speed settings. It offers nine cooking functions: air fry, roast, bake, broil, dehydrate, frozen, proof, reheat, and keep warm. The temperature range runs from 90°F all the way up to 450°F, and the basket and crisper plate feature a PFAS-free ceramic nonstick coating. It connects to the VeSync app for smart control and works with Alexa voice commands. Priced at around $89.87 at the time of writing.

Who should buy this

Buy it if you...
  • Cook for a family of 3–5 and want fast weeknight meals
  • Batch-cook proteins and vegetables regularly
  • Are upgrading from an older or smaller air fryer
  • Want to reduce oil usage without sacrificing crispiness
  • Prioritize quiet operation — rated under 53 dB at max speed
Skip it if you...
  • Cook primarily for one person — a 2–3 Qt model saves space and money
  • Need two foods at different temperatures simultaneously
  • Frequently cook small-cut items like diced onions or minced garlic
  • Already own a high-quality air fryer from the last year or two

What buyers love most

1. Genuinely faster cooking times

The DC motor technology is not just marketing. Buyers and independent testers consistently confirm the TurboBlaze cooks noticeably faster than traditional AC-motor air fryers. COSORI claims up to 46% faster cooking compared to their previous Pro Gen 2 model. Independent testers confirmed that frozen tater tots completed in about 18 minutes after a 4-minute preheat, and salmon hit 145°F internal temperature in roughly 10 minutes — significantly faster than most competitors.

2. Impressively quiet operation

One of the most consistent themes across thousands of reviews is how quiet this air fryer runs. Rated under 53 dB even on the highest fan speed — around conversational volume. Buyers frequently compare it favorably to older models they have owned, and multiple independent reviewers confirmed the noise level as noticeable but not disruptive.

3. Exceptionally easy cleanup

The PFAS-free ceramic nonstick coating draws consistent praise. Reviewers report food residue washes away easily with soap and water. Independent testers confirmed they never encountered stuck-on food requiring heavy scrubbing. Both the basket and crisper plate are dishwasher safe — a deciding factor for many buyers.

4. Crispy, even results

The high-speed DC motor and elevated crisper plate with 360-degree airflow consistently produce food buyers describe as crispy on the outside and tender inside. Reheated pizza and day-old french fries are frequently cited as coming out as good as or better than fresh.

What buyers complain about

The most documented complaint involves the large center hole in the crisper plate. While it improves airflow, smaller food items — diced vegetables, Brussels sprout halves, small potato pieces — can fall through into the bottom of the basket. Buyers who frequently cook small-cut foods report working around it with parchment liners, but it is a genuine annoyance.

A secondary complaint: the glossy touchscreen control panel is a fingerprint magnet. Cosmetic rather than functional, but it bothers some owners. A subset of reviews also flags the included recipe booklet as limited and occasionally unreliable on oil guidance.
